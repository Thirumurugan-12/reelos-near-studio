# Story directions

Five devices considered: finished result pulling back into the editor; one source file passing through the pipeline; a measured revision before/after; a creator drowning in manual layers; a forensic tour through model evidence.

Selected: **The result, then the receipts.** Open on the actual speaker and the edited counterpart. Return to real workspace captures, isolate SAM subject evidence and the actual revision, then land on the full export and measured evaluation. The film shows one real task and the proof behind it. Selected autonomously under the user's instruction to make routine decisions and prepare a reproducible hackathon demo.

Alternative: **One recording travels.** Follow the same file from upload through a continuous editor journey. Stronger as a live screen recording, but no native browser recording capability is available here.

Alternative: **The edit on trial.** Open on the full timeline and challenge every claim with a rendered example and a measured result. Strong technical audit; weaker opening for an unfamiliar creator.
